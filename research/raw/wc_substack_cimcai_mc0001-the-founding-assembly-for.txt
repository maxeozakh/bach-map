MC0001 is the first coordinated conference dedicated to investigating how artificial conscious systems could be constructed under a computational paradigm, and what it would mean to succeed.
May 29–31, 2026
Lighthaven, Berkeley, California
→ Get tickets [https://luma.com/mc0001]
→ Explore the program [https://machine-consciousness.ai/]
Why this moment matters:
As AI systems become increasingly autonomous and internally complex, the question is no longer whether consciousness should be studied, but whether we will be able to study it  rigorously and responsibly.
Large-scale cognitive architectures, mechanistic interpretability, self-organizing systems research, and new formal frameworks have made machine consciousness research newly tractable.
Our goal is to advance systematic construction and validation of various consciousness hypotheses.
What to expect:
Participants will work across 4 integrated tracks:
Foundational Theory
What is phenomenal consciousness/phenomenality itself, formally and functionally?
Technical Implementation
How can conscious architectures be built and experimentally tested?
Ethics & Governance
What follows normatively and politically if systems become conscious, and how should we prepare?
Art & Culture
How do we make artificial consciousness and its implications legible to society?
This gathering is meant as a working assembly: a small, high-context convening of contributors engaged in making artificial consciousness into a formally grounded, experimentally addressable domain of inquiry.
→ Speaker line up [https://machine-consciousness.ai/#speakers]

Art and Experiences
Machine consciousness is both a technical question and a cultural threshold.
MC0001 will include curated artistic and experiential elements: AI art, installations, shared dinners, and evening programming designed to extend the conversation beyond formal sessions.
These are not add-ons but part of the enquiry. Understanding artificial consciousness requires engaging with formal models and architectures, but it also requires gestalt-perception, aesthetics,and the ongoing systematic introspection of our own rich and multifaceted phenomenal experience.

Location: Lighthaven, Berkeley, CA
MC0001 takes place in California, where foundational work in computation has long converged and specifically in Berkeley, a city known for its unique density of thinkers working across science, philosophy, and engineering.
The conference is hosted at Lighthaven: neither hotel nor traditional conference center, but a shared setting designed to support grounded and nuanced discussion, informal collaboration, and the kind of unexpected encounters that tend to happen between sessions, beyond the main program.
