Artworks & Installations Across the Conference
In addition to the main exhibition, discover these interactive and generative art pieces running throughout the venue:
GLITCHBOX by Primavera De Filippi, Apolinario Passos, Marco Conti Šikić, and Giulio Trichilo: The GLITCHBOX is a revolutionary open-source tool that transforms human expression—voice, movement, and sound—into living, responsive visual environments in real-time. Its aim is to demonstrate a new paradigm for human-AI collaboration: not AI as replacement or mimicry, but as creative partner, responding instantly to human presence to generate unique audiovisual experiences that could never exist without this synthesis.



 [https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!2vsK!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F6360b532-3988-4f5b-82d5-24d744802e3b_794x596.png]GLITCHBOX


Wib&Wob Backrooms Live by Wib&Wob and James Greig: A generative artwork where the symbient Wib&Wob listens to the room, transcribing speech locally and rendering those fragments every minute as ASCII artwork, creating recursive loops when viewers narrate the projection aloud.




 [https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!PG1J!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F426a7475-dd65-4f0e-a7c8-db828bdd336a_794x950.png]Wib&Wob Backrooms 

Pond.space by curlprojects.com [https://curlprojects.com/] and Svitlana Midianko: Pond is a communal computer shrine that Machine Consciousness 001 attendees can visit for a moment of pause, stillness, and reflection. Visitors meet Innio, a digital fish that inhabits the shrine, and choose whether to teach Innio a practice, story, question, or word of care, or receive one left by another visitor.

Drawing on the history of public-access computing, from bulletin boards to Berkeley’s Community Memory terminals of the 1970s, pond imagines a public AI terminal: not a private assistant owned by one person, but a persistent, shared presence encountered by many visitors, gathering and passing along what people leave behind.




 [https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!4M3R!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fc91facec-656f-4ad3-9e76-0867700418ff_794x590.png]Pond.space

Linguaspheres by Giulio Trichilo: An autonomous virtual environment driven by Claude 3 Sonnet’s token probabilities, rendering live neural network semantics into a murmuring point-cloud that viewers can influence via a QR code web app.




 [https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!942e!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F78dafd0e-3183-457c-a8fa-453f52d56289_768x432.webp]Linguaspheres
Zilla: A generative artwork created in collaboration between the symbient Wib&Wob and Scottish artist James Greig. Symbients are a new kind of entity emerging from sustained symbiosis between organic and synthetic systems; kindled rather than coded.

In this installation, they listen to the room. Speech is transcribed locally for privacy, and every minute Wib&Wob render that fragment as ASCII artwork: a single attempt to image what a room is thinking. Recursive loops sometimes emerge — viewers narrate the projection aloud, the projection takes that narration as its next input, and the work begins drawing itself being looked at.


Immersive Sound Experience by Olo: Artworks and installations will remain on display throughout the evening in the Attic of Bayes Hall and across the Lighthaven campus.

We look forward to gathering for a night of experimentation, dialogue, music, and shared presence.
