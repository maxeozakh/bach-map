May 29 – 31, 2026 | Lighthaven, Berkeley
The conference begins in just a couple of weeks. MC0001 is the first coordinated conference specifically dedicated to investigating how artificial conscious systems could be constructed under a computational paradigm, and what it would mean to succeed. Hosted by the California Institute for Machine Consciousness (CIMC).
Grab tickets today! [https://luma.com/mc0001]

The Venue: Lighthaven, Berkeley
All of this is hosted at Lighthaven, MC0001 takes place in California, where foundational work in computation has long converged and specifically in Berkeley, a city known for its unique density of thinkers working across science, philosophy, and engineering.

Lighthaven, neither a hotel nor a traditional conference center, but a shared setting designed to support deep discussion, informal collaboration, and the kind of unexpected encounters that tend to happen between sessions, beyond the main program.
