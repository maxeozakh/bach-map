how this was made

. made with an AI model, claude fable 5.2, as of 13 november 2026, and not checked by a person. joscha bach didn't make or approve it.

what each part is based on

  • which points are on the map: names and ideas found in transcripts of joscha bach's public interviews, talks and papers, and the CIMC site. the transcripts were searched by name, not read end to end, so the list is incomplete. it leans towards computational views of mind, and traditions bach rarely mentions are thin or missing
  • summaries: written by claude fable 5.2 from its general background knowledge about each idea. not bach's words, not checked against sources
  • connections: written by claude fable 5.2 from general knowledge, not taken from the transcripts. the least grounded part; read them as plausible, not established
  • quotes: the ones marked "verbatim" were matched against the transcripts; "paraphrase" ones are claude fable 5.2's rewording
  • years: approximate for older items
  • review: on 13 november 2026 claude fable 5.2 re-read every summary and connection in four separate passes, looking up doubtful claims on the web, and about 40 corrections were made (wrong by-lines, misstated positions, connections pointing the wrong way). most items were checked from the model's own knowledge, not against a source, and still no person has checked them
